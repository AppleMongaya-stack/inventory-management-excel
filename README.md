# 📦 Inventory Management & Stock Optimization

## 📌 Project Overview

An Excel-based inventory analytics project designed to help retail operations monitor inventory health, identify stock risks, and compare actual sales with forecast demand.

The dashboard transforms raw retail inventory data into actionable KPIs and visual insights for inventory planning and operational decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Questions

* How healthy is the current inventory?
* Which products are at risk of stockout?
* Which products have excessive inventory?
* How do actual sales compare with forecast demand?
* Which categories have high inventory but low sales?
* How do holidays and promotions affect sales?
* Which products require immediate attention?

## ⚠️ Inventory Health

Products are classified into different stock conditions:

| Stock Status | Description                                      |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Normal       | Inventory is within the expected range           |
| Low Stock    | Inventory is below forecast demand               |
| Overstock    | Inventory is significantly above forecast demand |
| Out of Stock | Inventory level is zero                          |

## 🔎 Analysis Areas

### 1. Inventory Health

Identifies the overall distribution of products across Normal, Low Stock, Overstock, and Out of Stock categories.

### 2. Actual Sales vs Forecast

Compares actual units sold with forecast demand to identify demand patterns and potential planning gaps.

### 3. Inventory vs Sales

Helps identify categories carrying high inventory relative to their sales performance.

### 4. Inventory Requiring Attention

Highlights products that may require replenishment, investigation, or inventory reduction.

### 5. Holiday / Promotion Impact

Compares sales performance between normal days and holiday/promotion periods.

## 💡 Business Value

The analysis can support inventory teams by helping them:

* Identify products requiring replenishment
* Detect potential stockout risks
* Reduce excess inventory
* Compare inventory levels with demand
* Prioritize products requiring operational attention
